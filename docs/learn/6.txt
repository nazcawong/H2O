Lesson 6  records

. is a field or a module, not a method.

A closed record {name: Text} cannot gain or drop fields.
{name: Text, ...R} may have extras.

  p = {name: "Ada", active: True}
  p.name
  p.active

Type aliases (in a file; the REPL accepts type too):

  type User = {name: Text, active: Bool}

Update fields by spreading; do not mutate the original:

  {p..., active: False}

Exercises:

  1) make {name: "Grace", age: 85} and print name
  2) :t p.name  (define p first)
  3) {p..., age: 86} and confirm p.age is still 85
